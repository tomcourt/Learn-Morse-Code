Privacy Disclosure

Learn Morse Code

At Learn Morse Code, we prioritize your privacy. The app runs locally on your device and does not require an account, use advertising trackers, or upload data to remote developer servers.

Because the app supports voice input to evaluate your answers during practice lessons, this disclosure explains how speech data is handled, what is written to system logs, and how diagnostic sharing and crash reports are affected.

Summary

Learn Morse Code processes spoken responses locally on your device to evaluate your answers. To help debug recognition timing and app crashes, diagnostic logs record recognition events. If you submit a bug report with logs attached or participate in Apple's "Share with App Developers" crash reporting, recent text transcriptions of recognized speech may be included in those diagnostic logs. Audio recordings are never saved or sent to external servers. You can opt out of attaching log files when reporting bugs or disable developer diagnostic sharing in your device's Privacy Settings.

Speech Recognition and Audio Data

  • Microphone access is requested solely to detect and evaluate your spoken answers against Morse code prompts in real time.
  • Raw audio is processed live and is never recorded, saved to storage, or transmitted to developer servers.
  • Speech recognition is performed on-device using Apple speech frameworks.

Diagnostic Logs, Crash Reports, and Spoken Transcriptions

During active practice sessions, the app writes status messages to the system log to help debug timing and recognition accuracy. These log entries include text transcriptions of words or phrases detected by the microphone.

Apple Crash Reports ("Share with App Developers")

If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in your iOS device settings, Apple may automatically collect recent system log entries when an unexpected crash occurs. If the app crashes while listening for speech, the diagnostic report sent to the developer may include transcriptions of what was spoken immediately before the crash.

In-App Support and Bug Reports

When using the in-app support feature to report a bug, the app offers an option to attach a recent diagnostic log file (system.log). If attached, this file will contain recent speech recognition entries and text transcriptions.

Privacy Controls and Choices

  • You can toggle off "Attach Log file" when submitting bug reports in the app, or inspect and edit the email attachment before sending.
  • You can prevent iOS from sharing crash reports and system logs with developers by going to Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements and disabling "Share with App Developers".
  • You can revoke microphone or speech recognition permissions at any time in Settings > Learn Morse.

Data Storage and Tracking

  • The app does not track you across apps or websites.
  • All settings, custom character sets, practice files, and tone configurations are stored only on your local device.
